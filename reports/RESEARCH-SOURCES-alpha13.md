# Research sources and design consequences — alpha.13

- **Grafana Node Graph documentation** — large layered graph layouts become expensive at hundreds of nodes; consequence: adaptive focus+context projection and disclosed LOD instead of insisting every entity render simultaneously.
- **Kiali topology documentation** — animation density/speed/error styling can represent admitted traffic state; consequence: Habitat particles/pulses are tied to actual activity/edge facts rather than decorative hacker text.
- **PixiJS renderer documentation** — WebGL is mature while WebGPU remains more experimental; consequence: retain self-contained Canvas2D at current measured scale and make GPU migration benchmark-triggered.
- **OpenTelemetry semantic conventions / GenAI guidance** — prompts, messages, tool arguments and related attributes may contain sensitive data; consequence: Runtime Twin redacts/bounds telemetry before persistence and Observatory projection.

Research informs architecture but does not substitute for Habitat regressions or product-quality A/B evidence.
